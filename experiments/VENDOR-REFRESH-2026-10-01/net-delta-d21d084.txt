    perf: keep count<0 check off the single-value record hot path
    
    hdr_record_value delegates to hdr_record_values, but the two are separate
    external symbols, so without LTO the count<0 guard added for the non-negative
    contract ran on every single-value record (not folded by count==1). On Zen 5
    this cost ~11% write throughput. Factor the record body into an internal helper
    and keep the count-sign check only in the explicit *_values entry points, so the
    hot path (count==1) is identical to the pre-guard code. Atomic twin kept in sync.
    
    Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 4.8 <noreply@anthropic.com>

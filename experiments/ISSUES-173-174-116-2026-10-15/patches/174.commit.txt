fix: fill unresolved hdr_value_at_percentiles slots like the singular

hdr_value_at_percentiles uses the caller's values[] as scratch space for
the cumulative-count target of each percentile. When the scan never
reaches a target, that target is left in the output slot. This happens on
an empty histogram, or when counts sum below total_count. On an empty
histogram every slot came back as 1.

After the scan, fill each unresolved slot with what hdr_value_at_percentile
returns when its own scan finds nothing: its not-found value 0, put through
the same p == 0.0 boundary rule (lowest_equivalent_value for p0,
highest_equivalent_value otherwise). The scan loops are unchanged.

Also correct the @return doc: null arguments give EINVAL, not ENOMEM.
